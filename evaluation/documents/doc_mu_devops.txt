Site Reliability Engineering Handbook: Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

1. Service Classification and Reliability Targets
Tier-1 production services (Authentication, Payment Gateway, Core API) are governed by a 99.99% availability SLO measured on a rolling 30-day window. The corresponding error budget is exactly 0.01% (approximately 4.32 minutes of downtime per month).

2. Incident Management and MTTR
Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) must not exceed 2.0 minutes for automated alerts. Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) for Severity-1 outages is targeted at 30 minutes. Automated rollback triggers if deployment failure rate exceeds 1.5% within 10 minutes of rollout.

3. Disaster Recovery and Replication
Database transactions are synchronously replicated across three Availability Zones. Disaster recovery testing requires an active-active cross-region Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 0 seconds and a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of under 15 minutes.
