Supersonic Transport Airframe Technical Review (Project Delta)

1. Aerodynamic and Structural Design
The Delta-IV commercial supersonic aircraft features an advanced modified cranked-arrow wing configuration. The primary structural fuselage utilizes Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy in high-thermal load forward sections and carbon-fiber reinforced bismaleimide composites for empennage surfaces.

2. Operational Performance Envelope
Cruising altitude is established between 55,000 feet and 60,000 feet at Mach 1.85. Maximum ferry range with full fuel reserves is 4,200 nautical miles. The aircraft accommodates 64 passengers in an all-business cabin layout.

3. Thermal Management and Avionics
Skin temperatures at Mach 1.85 reach up to 127 degrees Celsius along the leading edges. Avionics systems use redundant liquid hydrocarbon coolant loops dissipating 35 kilowatts of waste heat through wing-surface thermal radiators.
