Enterprise Information Security and Access Control Policy

Scope & Applicability:
This security policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing corporate infrastructure.

Authentication Requirements:
All accounts must utilize multi-factor authentication (MFA) via FIDO2 hardware tokens or approved authenticator apps.
Passwords must be a minimum of 16 characters and must be rotated every 90 days.
Failed login attempts are locked after 5 consecutive failures.

Data Retention & Auditing:
All database access logs and security event logs must be retained for a mandatory period of 7 years in immutable WORM storage.
Quarterly access reviews are conducted by the compliance department.
