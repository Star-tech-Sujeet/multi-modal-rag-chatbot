Superconducting Quantum Processor Specifications: Chip Xi-127

1. Architecture and Qubit Layout
The Xi-127 processor features 127 frequency-tunable transmon qubits arranged in a heavy-hexagonal lattice topology to minimize cross-talk and spurious capacitive coupling.

2. Coherence Times and Gate Fidelities
The median longitudinal relaxation time (T1) is 124 microseconds, and the transverse dephasing time (T2) is 98 microseconds across the active qubit array. Single-qubit Clifford gate fidelity is measured at 99.96%, and two-qubit cross-resonance gate fidelity averages 99.42%.

3. Cryogenic Operating Conditions
The quantum chip operates inside a dilution refrigerator at a base temperature of 14.5 millikelvin. RF control lines utilize superconducting niobium-titanium coaxial cables with 60 dB of total cryogenic attenuation.
