Aura Global Human Resources & Workplace Policy (Revision 2024-B)

1. Working Models and Remote Arrangements
Employees are eligible for fully remote, hybrid, or office-centric working models depending on organizational requirements. For all remote employees, core collaboration hours are designated from 10:00 UTC to 15:00 UTC Monday through Thursday.

2. Financial Stipends and Equipment
Full-time remote workers receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $1,500 upon joining. Additionally, a monthly connectivity reimbursement of $100 is provided to cover high-speed internet expenses.

3. Annual Leave and Wellness Days
Standard full-time vacation entitlement is 28 business days per calendar year. Up to 5 unused vacation days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31. The company observes 4 global mental wellness recharge days annually.
