Beta Corporation Annual Performance & Market Summary (2025)

Founding & History:
Beta Corporation was established in 2018 in Austin, Texas. The company develops cloud orchestration platforms.

Financial Metrics:
In fiscal year 2025, Beta Corporation reported total operating revenue of $15M and net income of $4M.
Operating expenses were $11M.
Beta Corporation holds $8M in liquid treasury assets.

Products & Services:
The core offering is BetaBot, an enterprise automation toolkit deployed across 250 corporate accounts.
BetaBot version 3.0 launched in early 2023.
