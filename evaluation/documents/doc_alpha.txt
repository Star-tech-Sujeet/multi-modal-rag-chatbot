Alpha Corporation Financial & Corporate Overview (2024)

Founding & History:
Alpha Corporation was founded in 2012 in Boston, Massachusetts. The company specializes in artificial intelligence software.

Financial Metrics:
In fiscal year 2024, Alpha Corporation reported total operating revenue of $10M and net income of $2M.
Research and development expenditures accounted for $3M.
The cash reserves at year end were $5M.

Products & Services:
The flagship product is AlphaBot, an autonomous robotics control system released in 2016.
AlphaBot currently serves 120 enterprise clients across North America.
