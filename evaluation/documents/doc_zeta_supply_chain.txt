Global Supply Chain and Fulfillment Report (Project Zeta)

1. Network Architecture
The Zeta distribution network operates four primary continental fulfillment centers located in Rotterdam, Dallas, Singapore, and Dubai. The network handles an annual shipping volume of 18.5 million metric tons of specialized industrial components.

2. Automation and Tracking
All tier-1 warehouses completed migration to autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and high-density automated storage and retrieval systems (ASRS). RFID tagging at the pallet and case level improved stock picking accuracy to 99.98%. Average order lead time was reduced from 14 days down to 6 days.

3. Resiliency and Risk Mitigation
Safety stock policies require maintaining 45 days of buffer inventory for critical semiconductor components. Dual-sourcing mandates dictate that no single component supplier may account for more than 60% of total procurement expenditure.
