Annual Financial Results & Operations Review: Fiscal Year 2024

1. Revenue and Earnings Performance
Consolidated revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 reached $248.6 million, representing a 14.2% year-over-year increase compared to $217.7 million in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 28.5%, generating $70.8 million in operating cash flow.

2. Capital Expenditures and R&D
Capital expenditures for the year totaled $12.8 million, primarily focused on server hardware upgrades and laboratory facilities. Research and development investments grew to $41.5 million, representing 16.7% of total revenue.

3. Balance Sheet and Liquidity
Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term marketable securities totaled $89.4 million at year-end. Total long-term debt remained at $15.0 million with a weighted average fixed interest rate of 4.25%.
