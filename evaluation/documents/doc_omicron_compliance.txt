Information Security & Regulatory Compliance Audit Report: SOC 2 / GDPR

1. Scope and Assessment Period
This independent audit report covers the observation period from January 1, 2024 to December 31, 2024, evaluating security, availability, and confidentiality trust services criteria.

2. Data Protection and Access Request SLA
Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) submitted under GDPR Article 15 are fulfilled with an average turnaround of 11 calendar days, well within the mandatory 30-day statutory deadline. Zero customer data breaches or unauthorized disclosures were recorded during the reporting cycle.

3. Cryptographic Controls
All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 with keys rotated annually via HashiCorp Vault. All data in transit across public networks enforces TLS version 1.3 with forward secrecy ciphers.
