Renewable Microgrid Engineering Specification (Project Gamma)

1. Executive Overview
Project Gamma operates a decentralized microgrid designed for autonomous islanded operation and grid parallel modes. The primary generation source is a 50 Megawatt bifacial monocrystalline solar array operating at an average module conversion efficiency of 23.4%.

2. Energy Storage Subsystem
Energy storage is provided by a lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) battery energy storage system (BESS) with an aggregate capacity of 120 Megawatt-hours (MWh). The battery management system enforces a maximum depth of discharge (DoD) of 85% to ensure a 15-year operational lifecycle. Inversion is handled by eight central PCS units, each rated at 6.25 Megavolt-amperes.

3. Grid Interconnection and Power Quality
The interconnection point ties into the 115 Kilovolt regional transmission network. Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) at the point of common coupling must remain strictly below 2.8% at full load. Emergency black-start sequence initiates within 450 milliseconds of transmission line disconnect.
